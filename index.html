ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

A conversation concept with real-time Unity avatar signing.

This interface demonstrates text, browser speech input, speech output and camera preview. The sign selector maps saved sign names to MediaPipe landmark clips and sends the selected name to the Unity WebGL avatar.
